FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Window Tint FAQ

Answers to the most common questions about our professional mobile window tinting services.

How long does window tint installation take?

Most vehicles are completed within 1.5 to 3 hours depending on the size of the vehicle and the number of windows being tinted.

How long should I wait before rolling down my windows?

We recommend keeping your windows closed for at least 48 hours after installation to allow the film to cure properly.

Does ceramic window tint block phone or GPS signals?

No. Premium ceramic films are non-metallic and will not interfere with GPS, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, or cellular signals.

Do you offer mobile installation?

Yes. We come directly to your home, workplace, or dealership throughout Durham Region and the GTA.

Is your window tint covered by a warranty?

Yes. Our premium films include a manufacturer's lifetime warranty against bubbling, peeling, cracking, and fading where applicable.

Which tint percentage should I choose?

We help every customer choose the best shade based on appearance, privacy, visibility, and Ontario regulations.
